Herbarium Dock

A vertical toolbar of twelve pressed-flower prints, one birth flower for each month of 2026. The pointer works like a magnifying glass: the print under it swells and its neighbours ease aside. Months already filed are grey impressions, the open month is struck in silver foil, and the rest wait in silver outline.

Button

States

Outline is the default. Hover magnifies the print and lifts a grey shadow print off the page beneath it. Press brings the stamp down: the shadow closes up under the print and silver starts to transfer. The chosen month stays struck in foil. Months before it are filed as grey impressions, like the stones already placed on the stone calendar.

Configurations

One structure, nine configurations. Alignment sets which edge the strip sits on and which way the prints grow: away from the wall when docked left or right, evenly both ways when centred. The magnifier is the same for every button type.

The magnifier

Scale is a cosine bump centred on the pointer. A print directly under the pointer reaches full size, and the effect fades to nothing 2.8 slots away. The stack then shifts so the point under the cursor stays still, so the print you are aiming at never slides away from you.

System

Structure

  • Strip: one ruled paper strip, 12 px inner padding, 6 px between slots
  • Icon slot 40 × 40, text pebble 57 × 36, icon + text 140 × 36
  • Each slot holds four stacked prints: shadow, impression, foil, outline
  • Tooltip slip: 178 px, attached by a 14 px hairline

Alignment

  • Left: strip on the left wall, prints grow right, slip on the right
  • Right: mirrored, and labels align right
  • Center: strip between plate and ledger, prints grow both ways

Motion

  • Magnify: max 2.15× icon, 1.75× text, 1.85× icon + text
  • Falloff radius 2.8 slots, cosine; gaps grow 60% as much as prints
  • Pointer smoothed with τ 45 ms; magnifier fades in and out with τ 85 ms
  • Press 0.9×, τ 50 ms. Selecting strikes the foil in over 560 ms (overshoot 1.14 → .95 → 1)

Keys & touch

  • ↑ ↓ move along the strip, Home/End
  • Focus also drives the magnifier, so the keyboard sees the same lens
  • Enter/Space files the month
  • Touch: drag along the strip to scrub, lift to choose
