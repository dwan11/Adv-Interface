# Jewellery Archive — current design

A single connected glass toolbar returns to the right side by default. Left and top-centred placements and icon/text display modes remain available. Search and outfit actions are removed from the visible composition.

The archive displays all 18 distinct existing jewellery designs together in an irregular arrangement inspired by the supplied archival sheet. Hover or focus on a category brings its three matching pieces forward from the mist. Clicking holds that category. Hovering or focusing a product reveals its category; product hover also highlights the corresponding toolbar icon. Clicking a product selects that design. Leaving a preview restores the selected category. Keyboard arrow navigation and the original sliding, clipped tooltip remain.

Palette: ivory #f3f0e7, sage #98a591, deep teal #2e544f, with opacity creating lighter and darker tones. Engravings use teal ink instead of the previous pink/blue mapping. Fog veils and per-object blur create varied focus and depth, including the products themselves. Water troughs remain pale. Reduced-motion disables animated transitions and ripples.

Validation: JavaScript syntax and diff whitespace checks. Browser visual verification was unavailable because local browser use was disabled in Work settings.
