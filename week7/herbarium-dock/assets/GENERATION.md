## Final generation prompt

Create a flat front-facing archival jewellery specimen asset sheet, landscape 3:2 ratio, pale beige paper #F5F1E8. Exactly two printing inks: moss green #596344 dominant 85% for all metal structure and shadows, antique ochre #A58B52 accent 15% only for gemstones and a few engraved details. No other colors. Vintage luxury through mechanical intaglio and halftone reproduction, not glossy rendering.

This is a production sprite sheet for an interactive archive: exactly THREE equal columns and TWO equal rows, six equal square cells, no visible grid. Each cell has ONE isolated jewellery object centered and entirely contained within the central 72% of its cell. Keep all six backgrounds identical clean pale beige with 40% blank paper overall. No object crossing cell boundaries. Top row left to right: an antique oval gemstone signet ring viewed three-quarter; a pair of ornate pear-drop earrings; an antique open cuff bracelet. Bottom row left to right: a sculptural bow brooch; an oval pendant on a short loose chain; a round vintage dress wristwatch with a dark engraved bracelet. All six objects need distinct recognizable silhouettes.

Render intricate engraved metal details with stippled shadows, coarse-to-fine halftone dots, clipped paper highlights inside each object and dry ink broken edges. Dimensional information comes only from ink density. Metal is printed green, not metallic gold or chrome. Gemstones are muted ochre ink, never bright jewels. Faithful object anatomy and recognizable antique craft.

No text anywhere, no labels, numbers, logos, titles or typography. The website adds its own live accessible typography, respecting the user's explicit request to remove unnecessary text.

Subtle paper fibers, 8% uneven ink density, 2% dry-edge breakup, 6% halftone drift in the objects only. No full-page dirt overlay, gradients, cast shadows, photography, mockup, 3D render, ornamental frame, flowers or decorative props. High resolution clean specimen assets.
