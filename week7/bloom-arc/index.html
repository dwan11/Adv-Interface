Bloom Arc

Tone: soft, liquid, playful. One round bud pours out a single bent pill of tools; choosing one pinches it off like a drop of water and lets it fall back into the bud. Function: pick one tool from a small set without leaving the canvas.

Tools

States

The toolbar is one continuous pill bent around the bud, with no seams. Hover swells a round bead out of the band whose edges thin smoothly back into it, like two drops of water touching. Press tightens the bead and fills an orchid disk; the current tool is a crisp plum disk sitting in its own bead.

Selecting: the drop

Choosing a tool cuts it out of the band. Its sides pinch in, the liquid neck stretches and snaps, the two cut ends recoil into rounded tips, and the freed drop falls back into the bud, which becomes that tool.

System

Structure

  • Bud: 56 px circle, shows the current tool
  • Band: one pill bent around the bud's centre, fully round ends (radius = half the band)
  • Band depth: 52 icon · 48 text · 64 icon + text
  • Each tool owns an equal slice; no dividers

Alignment

  • Center: arc up to 160°, symmetric over the bud
  • Left: bud in the left corner, arc sweeps 100° clockwise
  • Right: mirrored
  • Radius grows so each slice keeps ~70 px of arc

Motion

  • Open: slices fold out of the bud and fan to their angles on a spring (k 210, c 17), 34 ms apart
  • Liquid: blur 10 + alpha threshold on one lilac layer, so the band and its beads merge and split like water; colour sits on crisp disks above
  • Cut: pinch 0–250 ms, rise with a stretched neck 150–450 ms, ends snap back 360–580 ms, drop falls into the bud 520–920 ms

Keys

  • Enter/Space on the bud opens
  • ← → move along the arc, Home/End
  • Enter/Space chooses, Esc closes
