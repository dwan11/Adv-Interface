Bloom Arc

Tone: soft, playful, unhurried. The tools are folded inside one round bud and fan open above it like petals. Function: pick one tool from a small set without leaving the canvas.

Tap the bud to open the tools
Align
Button
Tools

States

One sector shape carries every button type. Hover lifts the petal outward, press pushes it in and flushes it orchid, and the chosen tool turns plum with a small notch pointing back at the bud.

System

Structure

  • Bud: 56 px round trigger, shows the current tool
  • Arc: n sectors around the bud's centre, 3 px seams
  • Band: 52 icon · 48 text · 64 icon + text
  • Rim line 9 px in from the inner edge

Alignment

  • Center: arc spans up to 160° symmetric over the bud
  • Left: bud in the left corner, arc sweeps 100° clockwise to the right
  • Right: mirrored
  • Radius grows so each petal keeps ~70 px of arc

Motion

  • Open: petals start folded at 12 o'clock at 36% scale and fan to their angles on a spring (k 210, c 17), 34 ms apart, from the middle outward
  • Close: they sweep back into the bud in 260 ms, ease-in
  • Hover 5 px lift · press 96% · selection closes after 280 ms

Keys

  • Enter/Space on the bud opens
  • ← → move along the arc, Home/End
  • Esc or a tap outside closes and returns focus
