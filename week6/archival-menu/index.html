Archival Menu

Specification

Set 01, Archival. A record is not performed, it is handled. So one idea drives every decision: the only thing that changes is ink on paper, and the only thing that moves is what a hand would move. The menu is a drawer you pull open, and all colour comes from the objects.

Two versions

Same rows, states and motion. Only the arrangement of drawers changes. Switch at the top of the page.

VersionArrangementStrengthTrade-off
VerticalOne drawer, fixed on the right. Three choices, then sub drawers for the 1800s and 1900sClosest to the required Apple menu structure. Figures stay in viewFewer ways to browse than the row of drawers
HorizontalFour drawers in a row. One opens at a time, floating over the page, and moving along the row opens the nextReads as a cabinet and a menu bar. Each object can be found several waysAn open drawer covers the note beneath it until it closes

Design intention

Each state is one step in handling a record. Motion, type and colour all answer to that step.

StateWhat is happeningMotionTypographyColour
EnabledThe record is in storageNone. Stored things are stillCourier Prime capitals, as typed on an index cardFaded ink, #63635C, so the list sits behind the objects
Hover / focusSomeone is typing the record upKeys strike one at a time, in an uneven human rhythm, with a small soft impactEach letter lands slightly off the baseline with uneven ink, as on a real machineReading ink, #34342E, at 84–100% density per letter
PressedThe record is markedSinks 0.6px while a rule draws left to right. Any keys not yet struck speed up and land as it passesSame letters, same place, same weight. Ink slowly bleeds at the edgesSlowly deepens from #34342E to #141310. Nothing new is introduced
SelectedIt is out on the tableThe rule stays, the three figures fade in from left to right, then the sheet is typedThe bled ink stays, with the hairline rule beneathFull ink against faded neighbours. Nothing is selected until you choose
WordplayA sheet has been typed about the object, and its note shows through from the backEach letter strikes in turn, as on hoverThe name repeats with letters missing and the rest spaced apart, on the monospaced gridInk takes the object's own colour, so the colour still comes only from the object
MenuThe drawer is pulled open, used, and pushed shutThe front travels and the contents move with it, emerging from under the cabinetA typed card names the drawer. Open drawer and Close drawer are typed key by key, like every labelLightbox tones only. The well is one step darker, as if in shade
SubmenuA smaller drawer is pulled from inside the firstIt extends beneath its row and pushes the front further out. Nothing overlapsSame row, name left, year right, sorted oldest firstStays faded. A drawer is opened, never selected, so it takes no rule
