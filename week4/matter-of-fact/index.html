Week 4 · Sliders · Study 01

Matter of Fact

One continuous and one stepped slider. Colors are fully opaque and there is no glow. The fill drifts slowly between nearby shades. The number appears only when you interact, and each change pauses so you can read it.

Continuous

0–100 · step 1
Volume35
  1. Default

    Cream thumb on a dark track, with no number.

  2. Hover

    A streak circles the thumb like a shooting star, and the value rises above it.

  3. Pressed

    The thumb grows 22% and the streak goes away.

  4. Drag

    Same as pressed. Digits change in short stop-motion steps and hold each number long enough to read.

Stepped

5 stops · 25
Brightness25
  1. Default

    A thinner track with one dot for each stop.

  2. Hover

    Same as continuous: orbiting streak and value.

  3. Pressed

    Same as continuous: the thumb enlarges.

  4. Drag

    The thumb glides to each stop and brakes gently, with a slight forward tip and a small overshoot. The fill blends from teal to magenta and keeps flowing at rest.

Color

  • Track solid, 100% opacity
  • Thumb cream, 1px drop shadow
  • Fill teal, slow flowing gradient
  • Fill teal → magenta by stop, 900ms blend

Motion

  • Hover streak orbits the thumb every 1.25s, value fades in over 160ms
  • Press scale 1.22, 240ms, small overshoot
  • Digits 3-frame flip (120ms), then hold ≥160ms while dragging
  • Drag (stepped) spring k 300 / c 28 (ζ ≈ 0.8), lean ≤ 4°

Access

  • Keys ← → step · Shift ×10 · Home / End
  • Role slider with value text
  • Reduced motion no springs, flips or flowing fill
  • Saved values are kept in this browser
