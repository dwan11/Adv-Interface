Week 8 · Honeydew Button Interactions
Amanda Wan · Desktop portfolio · Button system

Three levels, three gestures

The actions on each Playground card, in one Honeydew palette. A filled primary, a lower-contrast secondary and a transparent, outlined tertiary. All three share one logic: at rest they sit on the desk with no shadow; on hover they lift off, a shadow appears and the fill turns into a drifting Honeydew orb; pressed lifts them higher. Then each acts out its own gesture: the eyes smile and wink, the code decodes, Share launches data.

The set on each card

View design with icon and text, Code as text only, Share as icon only, pushed to the right edge so its “Link copied” never sits on Code. Click Code and it decodes to “Copied”.

Motion per state

Primary · View design

Hover: the eye opens and glances side to side; a honeydew glow appears. Pressed: the eye squints and the fill deepens.

Secondary · Code

Hover: brackets spread, a slash drops in, and the label decodes through ASCII glyphs. Pressed: brackets snap inward.

Secondary · Share

Hover: the Flare orb fills in and bits of data stream out. Pressed: the arrow launches and the bits burst. Done: a check draws itself.

Tertiary · Back to desktop

Hover: the chevron nudges left and the outline darkens. Pressed: it steps further left on a pale green fill.

Disabled

Every motion stops. Fills go pale grey-green, text drops to #B2B9A8, outlines fade to a hairline.
